Drain cleaning means removing a blockage from a drain line, either with a cable machine (snaking) or with high-pressure water (hydro jetting), so wastewater flows freely again. The right tool depends on what is blocking the pipe and how far down the line it sits.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Not every slow drain needs a midnight visit, but a few situations should not wait. The cards below show how most callers sort their situation before they dial.

Call right nowSewage or gray water rising in a tub, shower or floor drain, or several fixtures backing up at once.
Same dayOne fixture fully blocked, a kitchen sink that will not drain, or a washer that overflows into the standpipe.
Book a visitA sink or tub that drains slowly but still drains, or a gurgle that comes and goes.

What to do while you wait for the plumber

  • Stop using every sink, tub, shower, dishwasher and washing machine in the house.
  • Do not flush toilets if water is rising in the lowest drain, because the main line may be blocked.
  • Skip chemical drain openers. They can sit in the pipe and burn the plumber who opens it.
  • Move rugs and boxes away from the lowest floor drain, usually in the basement.

Why drains clog in Shakopee homes

Shakopee Public Utilities rates the city water at 21 grains per gallon, which it classes as very hard. Hard water leaves mineral scale on pipe walls over the years, so a drain line has less room for hair, soap and grease to pass. If your clogs keep returning in the same tub or sink, scale and soap buildup is a common reason, and a deeper cleaning usually lasts longer than a quick snake.

Kitchen lines clog with grease and food that cool and harden inside the pipe. Bathroom lines clog with hair bound together by soap. Older homes around downtown Shakopee can also have clay or cast iron lateral lines where roots find joints, which is why a clog on the main line often needs a camera look before anyone promises a fix. The sewer camera inspection page explains how that works.

Snaking or jetting: how the plumber chooses

MethodBest forLimits
Hand or drum snakeSingle-fixture clogs, hair, a toy or wipes stuck near the trapPunches a hole through the blockage but can leave grease on the pipe wall
Cable machine with cutting headMain line clogs and root intrusionCuts roots back but does not scour the whole pipe
Hydro jettingGrease, scale and repeat clogs, plus commercial linesNot for a collapsed or badly cracked pipe, which needs a camera first

Most residential clogs clear with a cable. When a line clogs again within a few weeks, the plumber will usually recommend hydro jetting because it cleans the pipe wall instead of only opening a channel.

Kitchen sink clogs

The kitchen sink is the most common call for drain work after the main line. A double sink that drains slowly on one side and backs up on the other usually points to a blockage past the shared tailpiece, not inside the disposal. Dishwasher drain hoses tie into the same line, so a dishwasher that leaves standing water is often part of the same clog.

Grease is the usual cause, along with coffee grounds and starchy food that swell in the trap. The plumber removes the trap, clears the wall pipe with a cable, and checks that the vent is not part of the problem. If the sink also has a disposal that hums or jams, the garbage disposal repair team handles that in the same visit.

Tubs, showers and floor drains

A tub that drains slowly is almost always hair and soap in the trap or just past it. Floor drains in basements and garages catch lint, sediment and sometimes sump overflow, and they are the first place a blocked main line shows itself. If water comes up in a floor drain when you run the washing machine, that is a main line signal, not a floor drain problem.

When the drain line itself leaks

Occasionally a clog is not the whole story. A damp ceiling under a bathroom, a wet spot on the basement wall near a stack, or a smell with no visible water can point to a leaking drain line. The plumber finds it with a camera and a listening check, and repairs the pipe section rather than clearing it over and over.
