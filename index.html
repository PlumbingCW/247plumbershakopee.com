First steps in any plumbing emergency

Do these while you wait for the plumber

  • Close the main water valve, usually near where the line enters the house or by the meter.
  • Switch off power to wet rooms at the breaker, and stay out of standing water near outlets.
  • Move belongings and rugs away from the water, and photograph the damage for insurance.
  • Stop using drains if water is backing up, and do not use chemical drain cleaner.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.

What local water does to your plumbing

Shakopee Public Utilities states that city water is 21 grains per gallon, which it classes as very hard. Hard water leaves scale in tank water heaters, clogs aerators and wears out fixtures, which is why water heater repair and water softener work come up so often in our area.

The numbers change from town to town. Chaska reports 26 grains, Prior Lake 19.3 and Chanhassen 21, while Eden Prairie softens its supply to about 5. The service areas page puts these side by side, and each town page explains what it means for repairs.

Hire a plumber you can check

Plumbers in Minnesota are licensed through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry, which provides a Check a License tool on its website. Ask the dispatcher for the name of the plumber being sent, and check it yourself. We would rather you verify us than take our word for it.

Plumbing code note

Plumbing work in Minnesota follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Minnesota Rules chapter 4714 and administered by the Department of Labor and Industry, which is why we ask about permits and code items on every job.
