Is your tank done? Signs it has failed

A tank that leaks from its shell or base is finished, and a replacement is the only fix. If you are looking at a puddle right now, shut the cold supply valve above the heater, turn off the power or gas, and call the 24/7 line. A plumber can quote the swap by phone and book the earliest slot, which is often the same day when stock is available.

Water heater replacement means draining and removing the old tank, setting a new one of the right size and fuel type, reconnecting water, gas or electric, venting and the relief valve line, and testing the unit before leaving.

Replace nowWater at the base from the tank itself, a bulging or rusted shell, or rust-colored hot water with a leak.
Plan within weeksA tank over 12 years old that now needs repeat repairs, or one that runs out of hot water mid-shower.
Watch and budgetA tank near 10 years old with no symptoms, where a planned swap beats an emergency one.

Why Shakopee tanks wear out

Shakopee Public Utilities reports the city supply at 21 grains per gallon. Hard water builds sediment at the bottom of a tank, and the burner or lower element then heats the sediment, not the water. That stress shortens the life of the steel and is the main reason a tank in a very hard water area may not reach the number on its warranty card.

An anode rod protects the steel by corroding first. Many homeowners never replace it, and by year six or seven it can be gone. A plumber can check the rod when called for a repair, and if you are replacing the tank, the new one should be paired with a plan for flushing.

Sizing the new tank

5 or more, or a soaking tub60 to 75 gallons, or tanklessA larger tank needs space and sometimes a different vent

The plumber will check the space, the vent and the gas line or breaker size before quoting. An undersized gas line or a 30 amp circuit on a unit that needs more can turn a simple swap into a bigger job, and you should hear that before work starts.

Replacement options beyond a like-for-like tank

Moving from a standard tank to a tankless unit changes the vent, the gas line and sometimes the electrical supply, so it is a larger project. The tankless water heater page covers the tradeoffs. A hybrid heat pump tank saves electricity but needs space and airflow, so it is a mention here and not a default.

If this is a planned upgrade and not an emergency, see water heater installation for the new-unit side of the work.

What to prepare before the plumber arrives

Quick prep that saves time

  • Clear a three foot path to the heater and take items off the floor around it.
  • Note the fuel type and the size printed on the old tank label.
  • Know where the cold water shutoff and gas valve or breaker are.
  • Move vehicles if the heater is in an attached garage.

Plumbing code note

A replacement heater has to meet the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, including a relief valve with a proper discharge pipe.
