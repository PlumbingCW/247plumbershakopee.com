Chaska has the hardest water on our map

The City of Chaska says in its 2025 drinking water report that the city has hard water at 26 grains. That is higher than the 21 grains per gallon Shakopee Public Utilities lists for Shakopee, and it explains why Chaska homes call about scaled water heaters, crusted faucets and clogged aerators. The city draws from seven wells between 333 and 817 feet deep and treats for iron, manganese and disinfection.

For a homeowner that means two practical things. A tank heater without a softener upstream will gather sediment faster, so water heater repair and flushing come up often. A softener sized for 26 grains also needs more frequent regeneration than one sized for 20, which the water softener page explains.

Downtown Chaska, brick and the river

Chaska began making brick in 1857 from local clay. Downtown still sits on the Minnesota River, and the older buildings and homes there have older plumbing than the newer parts of the city. Cast iron stacks, clay laterals and galvanized supply lines turn up in buildings of that age, and tree roots find old sewer joints in the older blocks.

If your downtown home or shop has slow drains in more than one fixture, start with a camera look at the lateral. Our sewer camera inspection page shows what the plumber looks for, and drain cleaning clears the blockage while you decide on a repair.

Jonathan and the freeway-era neighborhoods

Chaska introduced the Jonathan planned community in 1966, and the Highway 212 freeway brought a later population surge. That gives the city a mix of homes from the late 1960s onward through the newer subdivisions. Homes of that range often have copper supply lines with solder joints, and the earliest ones can show pinhole leaks. A leak behind a finished wall is a job for leak detection before anyone cuts drywall.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

Four steps while the plumber is on the way to Chaska

  • Close the main water valve, usually near where the line enters the house or by the meter.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker before you step into standing water.
  • Clear the area around the leak so the plumber has room and furniture stays dry.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher you are in Chaska, so the right crew is sent.
