Built in waves: what that means for Eden Prairie pipes

Flying Cloud Airport opened in Eden Prairie in 1946, and the city grew quickly once Interstate 494 and Eden Prairie Center arrived in the mid-1970s.

Homes from those decades often have copper supply lines, cast iron drain stacks and original water heaters that have outlived two or three replacements. Newer neighborhoods have PEX and PVC.

A plumber can read the era from the pipe. Original galvanized or older copper is a reason to think about repiping after a second leak, and a basement with a 1970s drain stack is a reason to check the line with a camera before major remodel work.

Softened city water changes the service calls

The City of Eden Prairie says its tap water averages 5 grains per gallon after treatment, down from about 21 grains straight from the wells, and that residents do not need a water softener. That is a real difference from Shakopee at 21 grains. Eden Prairie water heaters and faucets see less scale, so the calls we get lean more toward failures from age, pressure and leaks.

If you do run a home softener in Eden Prairie, a plumber can check its setting during any visit, and our water softener page covers settings in more detail.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak in Eden Prairie

What to do in the first minutes

  • Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise until the flow stops.
  • Switch off the breaker for any room where water is near outlets or appliances.
  • Clear furniture and rugs from the wet area so the plumber can work.
  • Call the 24/7 line and say you are in Eden Prairie with the cross street.

Places and the plumbing they use

Eden Prairie Center and the surrounding retail and office parks generate after-hours calls for restrooms, floor drains and water lines. Homes near Purgatory Creek, Lake Riley and Staring Lake can have damp lower levels in wet seasons, which makes a working sump pump important. See sump pump repair and commercial plumbing if either matches your situation.

The city reports that its 15 wells draw on the Jordan and Prairie du Chien aquifer group. If your house is older, ask the city what material your service line is made of, and test your tap if you are unsure.
