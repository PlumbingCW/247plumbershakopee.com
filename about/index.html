About Shakopee 24/7 Plumbing

We run a 24/7 plumbing dispatch line for Shakopee, built around one idea: a plumbing emergency should get a real answer at any hour.

Call (952) 866-9120

What we do

Shakopee 24/7 Plumbing answers calls around the clock for homes and businesses in Shakopee and the towns around it. The line is open at night, on weekends and on holidays, and a plumber is dispatched to the address once you describe what is happening. We handle emergencies such as burst pipes, backups and failed water heaters, and planned work such as installations, repiping and fixture replacement.

Our service list covers 21 services, from drain cleaning and water heater replacement to sewer line repair, boiler repair and commercial plumbing. The services index shows all of them.

How a call works

  1. You call the 24/7 line. A dispatcher asks where you are, what the water is doing and whether anyone is at risk.
  2. You get safety steps. We tell you how to shut off the water, what to switch off and what to leave alone while a plumber is on the way.
  3. A plumber is dispatched. Dispatch is available around the clock. We do not promise a fixed arrival time because traffic, weather and call volume change it.
  4. The plumber finds the cause. The plumber diagnoses the problem and explains the repair before work starts.
  5. You approve the work. You get the price and scope before the repair begins, and nothing is done without your agreement.

Where we work

We take calls for Shakopee and nearby towns. Dedicated pages cover Chaska, Eden Prairie, Prior Lake, Savage and Chanhassen, and the service areas page lists the rest of the area and the ZIP codes we check against.

Each town page uses the water facts that the local utility publishes, such as hardness and source, because local conditions change what plumbing problems look like. Shakopee water is 21 grains per gallon according to Shakopee Public Utilities, while Eden Prairie softens its supply to about 5 grains.

What we say and what we do not say

  • We tell you what we find and what it takes to fix it, in plain terms.
  • We do not promise a specific arrival time, because we cannot control traffic and weather.
  • We do not publish customer reviews or ratings on this site, because we would rather show you how we work than quote numbers.
  • We point to your utility and to the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry for facts we do not own, such as water quality and licensing.
  • We will tell you when a repair is not worth doing and when a replacement is the better option.

Emergency work and planned work

Emergency calls and planned jobs use the same phone number but run differently. An emergency call is triaged first, so the dispatcher can tell you how to limit damage while a plumber is on the way. A planned job such as a fixture replacement or a repiping project is scheduled for a time that suits you, with the scope and price agreed in advance.

Many homeowners find us during an emergency and then book the follow-up work, such as replacing the water heater that failed or adding a softener. That is a reasonable order of events, and we will tell you which jobs can wait and which cannot.

Who answers and who does the work

This website is a lead and dispatch service. Calls to the number on this site are answered by a dispatch line that connects you with local plumbing providers, and the plumber who visits your home may work for a provider other than the one that answered. Ask for the plumber's name, company, Minnesota license status and proof of insurance before you approve any work.

Licensing and how to check it

Plumbers in Minnesota are licensed through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. That agency provides a Check a License tool on its website, and you can use it to confirm the license of any plumber you hire. When you call, you can ask the dispatcher for the name of the plumber being sent, then check that name yourself.
