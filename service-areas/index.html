Shakopee and the five towns we cover most

Shakopee sits in the lower Minnesota River Valley, and the towns we serve most often are on the river or within a short drive of it. Our home base is Shakopee, ZIP 55379, where Shakopee Public Utilities supplies water at 21 grains per gallon, which it classes as very hard.

Each of the five town pages below describes the local water source and hardness as the city publishes it, the ZIP codes we dispatch to and the plumbing problems that fit the area. The numbers differ more than you might expect from town to town.

Why local water facts matter

Hard water scales up a water heater faster and clogs fixtures, while soft water leaves less to repair. Prior Lake and Chanhassen remove iron and manganese but do not soften, while Eden Prairie softens at the plant. If you are unsure about your tap, test it or ask your utility, and read our water softener page for sizing.

What to expect when you call from a nearby town

The dispatcher asks for your address first, because it decides which plumber is closest and what the drive looks like. You then describe the problem, and the dispatcher gives you safety steps before anything else. If you are in a town that is not on a dedicated page, the same process applies, and you will hear honestly whether we can reach you.

Lake and river towns have their own patterns. Homes near water often deal with sump pumps and high groundwater, while older homes in town centers deal with aging drain lines. Mentioning those details on the call helps the plumber bring the right equipment.
