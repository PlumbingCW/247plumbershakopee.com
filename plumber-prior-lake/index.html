Lake-level basements and the sump pump question

Prior Lake is built around Upper Prior Lake at 354 acres, Lower Prior Lake at 810 acres and Spring Lake at 290 acres. Homes close to the water often have lower levels that sit near the water table, and a sump pump is the thing between a dry basement and a flooded one. A pump that runs through a long wet season wears faster, and a battery backup is cheap compared with the cost of a ruined finished basement.

Check the pump, the float and the discharge line before spring, and read emergency sump pump repair for the checklist. If a basement already stays damp with a working pump, basement waterproofing is the next step.

Growth, Highway 13 and newer plumbing

Prior Lake had about 7,300 residents in 1980 and about 27,600 in 2020. The improved Highway 13 is credited with much of the recent growth, so a large share of homes date from the last four decades. Those homes mostly have PEX or copper supply lines and PVC drains, and the common calls are water heaters, softeners, disposals and the occasional builder-era fitting that fails.

Hard but treated: the city water

The City of Prior Lake lists total hardness at 19.3 grains per gallon, or 330 ppm, and says its treatment plant removes iron and manganese. The plant does not soften the water. The city draws from seven wells, five in the Jordan aquifer at about 345 to 372 feet and two deeper wells at about 630 feet.

At about 19 grains, scale shows on faucets and in tanks. A water heater on that supply benefits from a yearly flush, and a softener protects the whole house. A plumber can check the heater during any visit, and water heater repair covers the common failures.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak near the lake

First steps in Prior Lake

  • Shut off the main water valve, and if water is coming through a wall, shut it before anything else.
  • Turn off the breaker for the wet area, especially in a lower level near the pit or the washer.
  • Pull belongings away from the leak and photograph the damage for insurance.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher you are in Prior Lake and whether the basement has a pump.
