Terms of Use

The basic terms for using this website and our phone line.

Using this website

This website gives general information about plumbing and our services. It is not a substitute for a licensed plumber inspecting your property, and you should not treat any guide as professional advice for your specific situation.

Service and pricing

We give you the price and scope for a repair before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. Prices depend on what the plumber finds, so any figure on this site is general information, not an offer.

Arrival times

We dispatch as quickly as we can, but we do not guarantee an arrival time, because traffic, weather and call volume vary.

Third-party information

We refer to facts published by utilities and cities, and we name the source. Those facts can change, so check with the utility or city before you rely on them for a purchase or a repair.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Contact

Questions about these terms can go to contact@247plumbershakopee.com or (952) 866-9120.
