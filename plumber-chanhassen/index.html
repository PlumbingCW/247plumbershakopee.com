Burst pipes and freezes in lake-country homes

Chanhassen has lakes such as Lake Ann and Lake Minnewashta, and many homes near them are seasonal in feel even when they are lived in all year. Vacation-style cabins, lakeside additions and detached garages with water lines are the places pipes freeze, because heat does not reach them in a cold snap. A burst there floods before anyone is home to notice.

The best protection is a plan before the cold. See burst pipe repair for steps to prevent a freeze and what to do if one splits. A leak that is not obvious can be traced through leak detection before walls are cut open.

Same hardness as Shakopee, a different system

The City of Chanhassen states its water hardness is 21 grains per gallon, the same figure Shakopee Public Utilities lists for Shakopee. Its East and West water treatment plants remove iron and manganese but do not soften the water, so scale still forms on elements and fixtures. A tank heater here benefits from the same yearly flush as one in Shakopee.

If you have a water softener already, a plumber can check the settings and the brine tank during a visit. If you do not, the water softener page covers choices, and water heater replacement shows how a failed tank is handled.

Growth since 1990 and what it left behind

Chanhassen became a village in 1896, merged with the township to form a city in 1967, and has grown more than 120 percent since 1990, reaching 25,947 people in 2020. Most of the housing stock dates from that growth. Homes from the 1990s and 2000s have copper and PEX supply lines and PVC drains, and typical calls are fixtures, disposals, softeners and water heaters at the 15 year mark.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak in Chanhassen

The first minutes

  • Close the main shutoff valve, or the lake house valve if the property has a separate one.
  • Switch off the breaker for rooms where water is near outlets, a panel or appliances.
  • Clear the area and lift belongings out of the water path.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher you are in Chanhassen, with the cross street or lake name.

Plumbing code note
